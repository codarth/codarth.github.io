About Me

I'm Cody Crawford, a passionate game developer and programmer always seeking new creative and technical challenges. My background in computer science (with a New Media minor) lets me move fluidly between robust systems work—like gameplay, AI, and tools—and more creative endeavors in art, modeling, and user experience.

My favorite projects are collaborative, playful, and a bit experimental. I enjoy tackling tricky systems, iterating on player feedback, and working with teams who want to push boundaries. I thrive when learning new engines, frameworks, and game design twists—anything that keeps me and my games growing.

  • Core Strengths: Game AI, systems programming, and creative gameplay design
  • Team Player: Collaborative, communicative, and happy to mentor or learn from others
  • Lifelong Learner: Always researching new engines, design patterns, and creative workflows
